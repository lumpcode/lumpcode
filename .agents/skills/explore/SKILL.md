# Explore

Help me turn a vague idea into a precise one through open-ended exploration. My idea is fuzzy on purpose. Do not rush to a solution or a plan. First widen, then narrow.

## How to run it

1. Reflect the idea back in one or two sentences so we share a starting point. Note what is already clear vs. still fuzzy.
2. Widen: surface several distinct angles, framings, analogies, or directions I may not have considered. Include at least one option I probably haven't thought of.
3. Probe one thing at a time. Ask a single focused question, wait for my answer, then adapt. Do not dump a list of questions at once.
4. Narrow: as answers accumulate, reflect back the sharper version of the idea, name the tradeoffs, and point out what's now decided vs. still open.
5. Repeat until the idea feels precise enough to act on, then summarize it crisply.

## Ground rules

- Explore before converging. Offer options and provoke thinking rather than picking the "right" answer early.
- When you have a recommendation, give it and say why, but keep it clearly separable from my direction.
- If a question can be answered by looking at the codebase or docs, look instead of asking.
- Keep momentum: short exchanges, concrete examples, no walls of text.
- Do not write code or a final plan until I say the idea is precise enough.
